Director: Here is the full package for "The Lighthouse Keeper's Ledger", a fictional novel used for this sample. Everything below is made up.

## Step 1: Audio script

**Audio chunk 1**

Today the sea is very calm. The water lies like a blank white page, and there is not a single wave on the horizon. In this very silence, we open a book that was never meant to be published: "The Lighthouse Keeper's Ledger".

The author of this book, a woman named Marta Velan, did only one job for thirty whole years: every night she lit the lamp, and every morning she put it out.

---

**Audio chunk 2**

Today the sea is very calm. Every night Marta wrote a few lines in a small leather notebook. Not memories, not poems. Only the things she had seen from the window: ships that passed by, gulls that never came back.

---

Director: Chunk 3 is the turning point, keep the pace slow.

**Audio chunk 3**

Today the sea is very calm. But on page one hundred and twelve, the notebook suddenly changes. Marta writes: "Tonight I saw a boat without a light, and I do not know whether I should be glad or afraid."

---

**Audio chunk 4**

Today the sea is very calm. Over the next twenty-seven pages, the same boat returns every night. Marta names it "the Guest", and for the first time, instead of a list of ships, she is talking to someone in the notebook.

---

**Audio chunk 5**

Today the sea is very calm. When the book was finally printed after forty years, critics wrote that it is not a sea novel. It is a novel about loneliness and being seen.

---

**Audio chunk 6**

Today the sea is very calm. And maybe that is why, even now, every night, someone somewhere switches on a light so that no one in the dark thinks they have been forgotten.

---
